## Verification notes

`_validate_legal_citations()` uses `Counter(actual_roles) ==
Counter(expected_shape)`.  The retained total-count comparison is only
defense in depth and cannot weaken that equality.  Independent temporary-pack
probes confirmed rejection of helmet plus signal, helmet plus points,
red-light missing signal, red-light extra primary, red-light extra
well-formed signal-location, and the unknown `procedural_guidance` role.
The last fails first at Pydantic's `CitationRole` `Literal`; known malformed
role combinations fail at `TrafficSourcePack` validation.

The real pack loads with the required 3/3/1 citation shapes and keeps the
phone rule disabled.  The exact HIGH-01 sentence and the shorter denial are
both parametrized pytest cases; their responses have no curated trust level
or sources.

The worktree was already dirty with declared Public Beta and earlier-phase
material.  The Round 2 correction-owned implementation is confined to the
declared source-pack and two test paths; no Round 2 change was attributed to
traffic data, selector/runtime behavior, frontend, or MODE_2D.  The index was
empty and no Git operation was in progress.

The single LOW finding is report accuracy: the Round 2 report says “all 8
hard constraints 0,” while its hard-constraint block lists nine metrics,
including `INCORRECT_TOPIC_CITATION_SHAPES_ACCEPTED`.  The legal-beta runner
independently reports the first eight as zero, and the ninth is independently
zero through the focused tests and direct malformed-pack probes.  This is a
count-label/reporting defect, not a safety or runtime defect.
